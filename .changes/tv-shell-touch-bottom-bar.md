On Android phones held upright, a bar at the bottom of the screen replaces the row of icons at the top: Home, Sources, Play, Favorites and Recent. Play shows where you are and opens Movies, Live TV and Series (or a source's own sections) with one tap. Settings is a gear in the header. The bar steps aside while the keyboard is open, and Back closes the Play menu before going back.
